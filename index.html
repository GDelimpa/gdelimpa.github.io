ABOUT ME

I am a Senior Research Scientist at AI4I (RIAS Lab, directed by Luca Laurenti). I am active in the field of control theory and its intersections with formal methods, optimization and information theory.

Explore publications ↗Download CV ↓

Research interests

01 /
01

Formal methods & dynamical systems

hybrid systems (systems with both continuous and discrete dynamics), finite models of dynamical systems (finite abstractions), verification and control of dynamical systems through finite abstractions, formal languages and automata.

02

Stochastics & control

Markov processes, reinforcement learning, and information theory in control.

03

Optimization & control

nonsmooth dynamics, optimization-based control for safety-critical tasks (control-barrier functions, projection-based control), and feedback optimization (optimization algorithms interconnected with dynamical systems).

Academic background

02 /

I obtained the Diploma (joint BSc. and MSc.) in Electrical and Computer Engineering, from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA) in 2017. I obtained my PhD, Cum Laude (top 5% in the Netherlands), from Delft University of Technology, in 2022. I have previously held postdoctoral positions at TU Delft and TU Eindhoven.
